Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026 · Product: Manager Schedule Builder Pro (“Schedule Pro”)

Short version: Schedule Pro is offline-first. Your schedules, names, and preferences stay on your device. We do not operate a backend that collects your staff data.

1. Who we are

Schedule Pro is a retail manager scheduling tool. Contact: b.ralston62989@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

The app stores data locally in your browser or device (for example using localStorage), including:

  • Store name and number
  • Manager and key carrier names
  • Shift requests, time off, preferences, and generated schedules
  • App settings (active tab, dismissed banners)

This information is used only to run the scheduler on your device.

3. What we do not collect

  • No required account or login
  • No staff biometric data
  • No automatic upload of schedules to our servers
  • No sale of personal information

4. Network use

When online, the app may load optional libraries (for example spreadsheet export) from a content delivery network and may check for app updates via a service worker. Schedule content itself is not uploaded by the app.

If you install from Google Play via a Trusted Web Activity, Google Play services may process standard install and update data under Google’s policies.

5. Exports you create

Word, Excel, print, and JSON backup files are created on your device at your request. You control where those files are shared or stored.

6. Children’s privacy

Schedule Pro is intended for adult retail managers and business use. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Data retention & deletion

Data remains on your device until you clear site data, uninstall the app, or use in-app clear/reset actions. Uninstalling or clearing browser storage deletes local schedules.

8. Security

Because data is local, protect access to your device and browser profile. Use device lock screens and avoid shared unsupervised kiosks for sensitive staff information.

9. International users

Processing occurs on your device. If you later enable optional cloud features, this policy will be updated before those features collect data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: b.ralston62989@gmail.com.
