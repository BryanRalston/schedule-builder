Full description

Schedule Pro is an offline retail manager scheduler for store managers and key carriers.
NRF 4-5-4 periods, PTO locks, generate-then-edit. No login.

WHO IT'S FOR
Store managers and multi-unit leaders who schedule assistant managers and key carriers.

WHAT YOU GET
• NRF 4-5-4 fiscal period calendar
• PTO, RTO, and LOA treated as hard locks
• Fairness and weekend balancing as preferences — always review before you post
• Clopen-avoidance as a preference, not a guarantee
• Generate, then edit or swap shifts on the board
• Word, Excel, print, and JSON backup
• Works offline — data stays on the device

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set up your team and store period
2. Enter time-off and shift requests
3. Tune preferences and role rules
4. Generate, review the sheet, export

PRICE
Paid Google Play app: $19.99 one-time. Two free generates are included so you can try the engine. Pro unlocks unlimited builds plus Word/Excel.

No account required. No cloud sync. Sign-in is optional and not required for core use.

Always review generated schedules for policy and labor compliance before posting.
